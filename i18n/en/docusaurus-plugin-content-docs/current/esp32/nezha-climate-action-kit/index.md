# Climate Action Kit

The Climate Action Kit is an educational product designed for K–12 climate education, environmental monitoring, and hands-on programming activities.

Students can collect climate data using environmental sensors and program the Nezha For ESP32 Board to control fans, water gates, and other actuators. Through these activities, students explore the relationship between climate sensing, programming, and climate action.
