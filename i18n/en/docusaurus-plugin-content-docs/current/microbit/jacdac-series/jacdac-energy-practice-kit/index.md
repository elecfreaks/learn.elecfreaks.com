# Jacdac Energy Experiment Kit

## Introduction
The Jacdac Energy Experiment Kit is purpose-built for STEAM education. Leveraging a plug-and-play ecosystem, it makes abstract energy data clearly visible. Through PBL (Project-Based Learning), it guides students to solve real-world energy-saving problems like engineers, cultivating a mindset of sustainable development through interdisciplinary practice and empowering them to become future climate action leaders.
