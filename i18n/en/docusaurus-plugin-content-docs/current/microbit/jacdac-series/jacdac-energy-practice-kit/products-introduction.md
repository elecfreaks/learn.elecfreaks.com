# Jacdac Energy Practice Kit

---

## Introduction
The Jacdac Energy Practice Kit is purpose-built for STEAM education. Leveraging a plug-and-play ecosystem, it makes abstract energy data clearly visible. Through PBL (Project-Based Learning), it guides students to solve real-world energy-saving problems like engineers, cultivating a mindset of sustainable development through interdisciplinary practice and empowering them to become future climate action leaders.

## Product Features

1. Supports plug-and-play functionality, allowing modules to be added or removed at any time during operation without restarting the main controller. The system automatically identifies sensor types and provides real-time sensor status feedback.
2. Jacdac's diverse range of sensors, actuators, and other electronic modules adhere to unified hardware and software design standards, enabling flexible combinations that help users rapidly build projects.
3. Compatible with building-block expansion design, enabling free-form assembly.
4. Offers 16 engaging cases covering wind energy, hydropower, solar energy, and more — exploring energy conversion relationships while cultivating environmental awareness and conservation habits through hands-on models.
