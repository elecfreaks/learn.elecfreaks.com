# Product Introduction

## Introduction

The micro:bit Petal Smart Farm Explorer Kit is a smart agriculture STEAM kit for upper primary and lower secondary classrooms and clubs. It uses micro:bit as the controller and real planting as the learning experience. Its twelve progressive lessons cover greenhouse assembly, planting, environmental sensing, automatic control, cloud data and remote irrigation.

**Build your own smart farm with micro:bit and explore programming and the Internet of Things through real growing activities.**

## Hardware Introduction

### Petal Soil Moisture Sensor

Read the soil moisture value from 0 to 100 to understand water conditions around plant roots. Combine it with the water pump for automatic irrigation that responds to dry and wet soil.

### Petal Water Pump Module

Control the pump with micro:bit for smart irrigation. It supports local automatic watering and remote control through a webpage.

### Petal WiFi Module

Connect the farm to a network. Upload temperature, humidity, soil and light data to the cloud at intervals, view charts remotely, and send control commands from a webpage.

### Petal Rainbow RGB

A programmable full-color LED supports supplementary lighting, status indication and environmental alerts. Add light when the surroundings are dim, or show red when temperature or humidity is abnormal.

### Petal Temperature and RH Sensor

Monitor greenhouse air temperature and humidity in real time. Combine the readings with the OLED and alert logic to build a miniature weather station.

### Petal OLED Display

Display soil moisture, temperature, humidity and light readings. Turn environmental data into clear, visible information.
