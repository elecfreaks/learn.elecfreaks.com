# Face Detection

**Project: Face-Tracking Pan-Tilt Camera**

How It Works: When AI Lens Pro detects a face, the pan-tilt mechanism automatically controls its motors to follow the face's movement, keeping it as close to the center of the camera view as possible.

**Note: Make sure AI Lens Pro is powered on before running the program so that the camera can initialize properly and switch to the face detection app required for this project.**
