# AI Lens Pro

## Product Overview

AI Lens Pro is an AI-powered vision tool designed for classroom learning, creative STEM projects, and robotics competitions.

Its 180° flip camera supports flexible viewing angles for a wide range of applications—from overhead line-following navigation to close-up, front-facing AI experiments. With built-in Wi-Fi and an onboard power module, AI Lens Pro can operate wirelessly without external power or data cables.

This streamlined, cable-free design keeps classroom workspaces neat while making project setup and competition debugging faster and easier.
