# Product Introduction

AI Lens Pro is an AI vision tool purpose-built for educational instruction and STEM competition scenarios. Featuring a 180° reversible lens, it allows flexible switching between overhead shots for robot line tracking and close-up angles for hands-on training. The body integrates built-in WiFi and an independent power module, enabling wireless standalone operation without external power or data cables. The minimalist, wiring-free design keeps classroom desktops tidy and makes on-site debugging more efficient and hassle-free.
